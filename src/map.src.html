Markmap

For markmap.js.org and other programs: a tree of headings and lists; positions, colours, notes and further links ride along in its front matter.

Example

Use

  • Tap a node: its details open (on the right on wide screens, from below on phones): name, group, links, note, and the actions. The grey dot on its corner opens its note; it is dark when there is one.
  • A selected node shows a blue handle on its right: drag it onto another node to link the two, or onto free space for a new linked node.
  • Drag a node to move it; it then stays where you put it, and layouts leave it. Drag the canvas to move the view; pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Groups are in the panel (☰ on smaller screens): tap one to hide or show it, press and hold to show only this one. Edit renames them and changes their colours.
  • On a computer the two side columns fold away with ☰ (left) and the column symbol next to Settings (right), or both at once with the key \, so that the map takes the whole window.
  • Link to … in the details links the node to the next one you tap, as the handle does by dragging.
  • Text shows the map as lines to read, select, copy and edit; Apply (or going back to Map) takes the edit over.
  • Arrange → Compact (in the left column, or on the map on smaller screens) closes the gaps and keeps the order; Untangle leaves pinned nodes where they are. A node is pinned only on purpose: a click on the pin of the selected node, or Keep in place in its details.
  • With a mouse: double-click on the canvas for a new node, Ctrl/Cmd+click to select several, Shift+drag from a node to link it. Keys: 0 fits the map, Delete removes the selection, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes.
